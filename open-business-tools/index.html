Principles

Useful software should stay understandable.

Open Business Tools brings focused desktop applications together under one clear collection. Each app solves a practical problem without turning the workflow into a larger platform than it needs to be.

The tools are designed to keep everyday work, files and information close to the people using them. They share an emphasis on clarity, deliberate choices and dependable local workflows.

01

Practical by design

Focused applications for recognizable tasks, with complexity kept behind a clear interface.

02

Local-first

Work stays on the computer whenever the task can be completed without an external service.

03

Open source

Public source code makes the tools inspectable, adaptable and independent from a closed ecosystem.

Why it exists

Clarity and control should be available to everyone.

The collection starts from the practical needs of small businesses, where time, simplicity and control over information matter every day.

Those same needs are not limited to one audience. The applications remain available to individuals, teams and organizations looking for understandable tools and greater digital independence.
